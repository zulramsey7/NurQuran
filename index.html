2. Kebenaran Lokasi (GPS)

Aplikasi memohon kebenaran lokasi (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION dan ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) semata-mata untuk tujuan berikut:

2. Location Permissions (GPS)

The application requests location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION) solely for the following functionalities:

  • Calculating precise prayer times according to official Malaysian JAKIM zones based on your coordinates.
  • Determining the exact Qibla compass bearing from your current position toward the Kaaba in Makkah.

* Your GPS coordinates are processed strictly on your local device and are NEVER transmitted, stored on external servers, or shared with third parties.

3. Notifications & Prayer Alarms

We use POST_NOTIFICATIONS and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM permissions to deliver timely prayer alerts and play Adhan audio. You may enable or disable these notifications anytime via the app settings.

4. Local Data Storage

Your reading history, bookmarks, Khatam tracker progress, digital Tasbih counts, and display font preferences are stored exclusively on your device's local memory (AsyncStorage). We do not harvest personal identifying data without your explicit consent.

5. Secure In-App Purchases (NurQuran PRO)

All financial transactions for the NurQuran PRO lifetime license are processed securely by Google Play Billing or Huawei In-App Purchases (IAP). Neither NurQuran nor the developer collects or stores your payment card credentials or financial data.

6. Data Deletion Rights

You have full ownership of your data. You may delete all bookmarks, dhikr records, and reading progress at any time directly through Profile > Factory Reset Data within the application.
